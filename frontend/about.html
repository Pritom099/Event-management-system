A little about us

Life happens
in the room.

Gather is a home for independent event hosts and people curious enough to show up. We make it easier to find local experiences with care behind them.

Our point of view

The best plans
leave room for people.

A workshop that changes your Saturday. A talk that stays with you on the walk home. A table where nobody checks the time. We help those gatherings find their people.

For hosts, Gather offers a practical place to publish events and manage reservations. For attendees, it keeps discovery and tickets in one clear place.
